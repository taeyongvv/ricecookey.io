The studio

A small kitchen.

RiceCookey is an independent team that likes to keep things small. We wear a lot of hats — design, code, art, and sound — so every release carries one consistent point of view from start to finish.

We're based in Korea and build for players and people everywhere. We'd rather ship something small and well-made than something big and forgettable, then keep improving it batch after batch.

  • By hand

    Crafted, not assembled. Details get the time they deserve.

  • Small on purpose

    Tight scope, clear ideas, no filler. Less, but better.

  • Built to last

    We ship, listen, and refine — long after release day.
